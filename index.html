Privacy

The private version isn't a setting.
It's the architecture.

Most assistants promise not to misuse what you send them. Sunday is built so there's nothing to send.

  • No account, no sign-up

    There's no login screen, because there's no server holding a profile of you. Install it and start typing.

  • Your files stay put

    Photos, recordings, videos, and PDFs are processed by the model on your device. They are never uploaded for analysis.

  • It works with no connection

    The model is on your phone, so it answers in a tunnel, on a plane, or anywhere the signal drops out.

  • The internet is opt-in

    Sunday can pull live results for news, scores, and prices — but only when you give it permission, and only at your discretion.

Under the hood

A real frontier model,
quantized to fit.

Google's Gemma 4 runs through MLX, Apple's array framework for Apple silicon — so the neural engine in your phone does the work.

Gemma 4 E2B (INT4)
Engine

Parameters

Quantization

Context

Vision fidelity, video sampling depth, temperature, and response length are all yours to tune. Sunday bundles Gemma 4 weights under Google's Gemma Terms of Use.
