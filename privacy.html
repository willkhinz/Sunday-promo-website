1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Sunday ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Sunday mobile application for iOS (the "App") and our promotional website at sunday-ai-web.web.app (the "Website"), collectively referred to as the "Services."

Sunday is a private, on-device artificial intelligence assistant. Our core architecture is designed around a single principle: your conversations are yours alone. The AI model runs entirely on your physical device. We do not operate cloud servers that process, store, or have access to your prompts, messages, or AI-generated responses.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, or by accessing the Website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Services.

3. AI System Disclosure

In accordance with the EU AI Act (Article 50, effective August 2, 2026), we disclose the following:

  • Sunday is an AI system. All responses generated within the App are produced by a large language model (Google Gemma 4, 2.5B parameter variant) running locally on your device.
  • Sunday's outputs are AI-generated content and should not be treated as human-written, professional advice, or verified facts. Large language models can produce inaccurate, incomplete, or biased outputs.
  • The AI model is not connected to the internet and does not access real-time information unless you explicitly enable the optional Web Search feature.
  • No human reviews, monitors, or has access to your conversations with Sunday.

6. Web Search Feature

When you explicitly enable the Web Search toggle in the App's settings, the AI may issue outbound network requests to retrieve real-time information. This is the only feature in the App that transmits user-generated content to external servers.

6.1 What Data Is Sent

  • Search query text: The specific search terms generated by the AI based on your conversation.
  • Your IP address: Automatically included in HTTP requests by your network connection. We do not add additional identifiers.
  • User-Agent string: A standard browser identifier header included in HTTP requests.

6.2 Which Services Receive Data

ServicePurposePrivacy Policy
DuckDuckGoGeneral web search (Instant Answer API & HTML results)duckduckgo.com/privacy
Open-MeteoWeather data (free, no API key required)open-meteo.com/en/terms
WikipediaEncyclopedia summaries (Wikimedia REST API)Wikimedia Privacy Policy

6.3 User Control

  • Web Search is disabled by default. No search queries are ever sent unless you explicitly enable the toggle.
  • You can disable Web Search at any time by toggling the setting off. All future queries will be processed entirely offline.
  • When Web Search is disabled, the App makes zero outbound network requests (except the one-time model download during initial setup).

10. Data Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your information:

  • On-device architecture: The most effective data protection is not collecting data in the first place. All AI processing occurs locally on your device's Apple Silicon GPU.
  • iOS App Sandbox: All app data is stored within iOS's sandboxed container, isolated from other apps and protected by iOS's file-level encryption.
  • HTTPS encryption: All network communications (model download, web search) use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.
  • No server-side storage: We do not operate backend servers that store user data. There is no database to breach.
  • No remote access: We have no mechanism to remotely access, view, or extract data from your device.

While no system is 100% secure, our on-device architecture significantly reduces the attack surface compared to cloud-based AI services.

15. Model Training & AI Data Use

Your data is never used to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model.

  • Sunday uses a pre-trained, static model (Google Gemma 4) that is downloaded once and runs locally. The model weights do not change based on your usage.
  • Your conversations, prompts, images, voice recordings, and AI responses are never transmitted to us, Google, or any third party for model training purposes.
  • We do not collect feedback data, thumbs-up/down ratings, or conversation quality signals for model improvement.
  • The model cannot "learn" from your conversations between sessions. Each session uses the same base model weights.
