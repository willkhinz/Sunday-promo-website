    Privacy

    The private version isn't a setting. It's the architecture.

    Most assistants promise not to misuse what you send them. Sunday is built so there's nothing to send.

    • No account, no sign-up

      There's no login screen, because there's no server holding a profile of you.

    • Your files stay put

      Photos, recordings, videos, and PDFs are processed on your device. They are never uploaded for analysis.

    • It works with no connection

      The model is on your phone, so it answers anywhere the signal drops out.

    • The internet is opt-in

      Sunday can pull live results for news, scores, and prices — but only when you give it permission.
